How to start

  1. Turn on Grades Dashboard for Schoology in Safari's Extensions settings.
  2. Open Schoology in Safari and sign in as you normally do.
  3. Click the Grades Dashboard button in Safari's toolbar. When Safari asks, choose Always Allow on This Website.

Keep a Schoology tab open when you refresh the dashboard.

Private by design

Your grades stay on this Mac. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. There is no account, no server and no ads, and your Schoology password is never asked for.
