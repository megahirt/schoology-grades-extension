How to start

Do these steps once. It takes about two minutes.

  1. Open the Settings app (the gray gear).
  2. Tap Apps, then Safari, then Extensions.
  3. Tap Grades Dashboard for Schoology and turn on Allow Extension.
  4. Open Safari and go to your Schoology page: app.schoology.com or schoology.npchristian.org
  5. Sign in to Schoology as you normally do.
  6. If Safari asks about Grades Dashboard, tap Always Allow on This Website.
  7. The dashboard opens over the page. Tap × to hide it. Tap the dark tab on the right edge to bring it back.

Optional: put Schoology on your Home Screen

  1. In Safari, on your Schoology page, tap the Share button (a square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Turn off Open as Web App, then tap Add.

Why off? Apple doesn't run Safari extensions in Home Screen web apps, so the dashboard won't show there. With it off, the icon opens Schoology in Safari, where the dashboard works.

Optional: tidy up

  1. You don't need to open this app again. To hide its icon, touch and hold it, tap Remove App, then Remove from Home Screen.

Don't choose Delete App. That removes the dashboard from Safari too.

How to start

  1. Turn on Grades Dashboard for Schoology in Safari's Extensions settings (the button above opens them).
  2. Open Schoology in Safari and sign in as you normally do: app.schoology.com or schoology.npchristian.org
  3. The dashboard opens on the right side of the Schoology page. If Safari asks about Grades Dashboard, choose Always Allow on This Website.

No dashboard? Click the Grades Dashboard button in Safari's toolbar and allow it on Schoology. Click × to hide the dashboard, and the dark tab on the right edge to bring it back. Keep a Schoology tab open when you refresh it.

Private by design

Your grades stay on this Macdevice. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. There is no account, no server and no ads, and your Schoology password is never asked for.
