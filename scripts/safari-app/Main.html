How to start

  1. Open Settings, then Apps, Safari, Extensions, and turn on Grades Dashboard for Schoology.
  2. Open Schoology in Safari and sign in as you normally do.
  3. Tap the Extensions button in Safari's address bar, then Grades Dashboard. When Safari asks, choose Always Allow on This Website.

Keep a Schoology tab open when you refresh the dashboard.

Private by design

Your grades stay on this Macdevice. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. There is no account, no server and no ads, and your Schoology password is never asked for.
