School address

Grades Dashboard works on app.schoology.com and every address ending in .schoology.com. Some schools sign students in at their own address, such as schoology.yourschool.org. If yours does, add it here. Your browser will ask to let the extension read that one site.

Copy it from the address bar while you're signed in to Schoology.

Other school addresses can't be added yet. Grades Dashboard works on addresses ending in .schoology.com and on schoology.npchristian.org.

Privacy & data

Grades are read from Schoology in this browser and stay here. Nothing is sent, and marking work turned in or done does not notify anyone.
