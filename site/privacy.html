Privacy policy

Homework Dashboard for Schoology (the browser extension) · Last updated November 8, 2026

The short version

The extension collects no data. Grades are read from Schoology in your browser and stay in your browser.

What the extension reads

When you're signed in to Schoology, the extension reads the Schoology grades and course pages your account can already see, using your browser's existing session. It never asks for or stores your Schoology password.

Where data is kept

Grades, settings, records, the daily log and your "turned in" and "done" marks are saved only in your browser's local extension storage on your device. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else. There are no analytics, ads or trackers.

Network requests

The extension only contacts your school's Schoology site (*.schoology.com), to read the pages you can already see.

Feedback

If you choose Send feedback, your own email app opens a message to the developer with the extension version and browser name. Nothing is sent unless you send that email.

Deleting your data

Settings → Clear data removes everything the extension saved. Uninstalling the extension also removes it.

Children's privacy

The extension is used by students and their parents. Because it collects and shares no personal information, nothing is collected from children.
