For teachers

What families see, and what it means for you

The dashboard shows families what you've already put in Schoology, organized so the important parts come first.

Your Missing marks matter most

The dashboard only calls something Missing when you mark it Missing in Schoology. A plain zero isn't Missing. When you clear the mark after late work comes in, it leaves the family's list.

Fewer surprises at report-card time

Weekly missing-work information for parents cut course failures in a large study.[1]

Paper work

Families can tick paper work as "turned in". That's only for them: it doesn't notify you or change anything in Schoology.
