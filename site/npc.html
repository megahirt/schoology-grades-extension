For NPC families

Set it up in five minutes

NPC uses Schoology, so the dashboard works with the Schoology login you already have. Pick the checklist that fits you.

Made by an NPC dad. This isn't an official NPC tool, and NPC hasn't endorsed it.

👪 Parents

  1. Use your own computer or phone, not your student's school MacBook. Install it for your browser.
  2. Open Schoology in that browser and sign in with your parent account, the same one you use for NPC's Schoology today.
  3. The dashboard tab appears on the right edge of the page. Click it, then press Refresh. It reads each of your children's grades.
  4. Open settings (the gear) and agree on the family rules with your student: the passing grade and how much missing work is too much.
  5. Look together once a week. Notice what's going well before what's missing.

🎒 Students

  1. On a school MacBook: it only works once NPC's IT has approved it. Until then, use your own phone or computer.
  2. iPhone: install it for Safari, turn it on in Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions, then open Schoology in Safari, not the Schoology app.
  3. Android: use Firefox, and open Schoology in Firefox.
  4. Sign in with your student Schoology account. You'll see what to finish first, what's due, this week's ring and your records.
  5. Your parents see the same information. You never see a GO/NO-GO verdict about yourself.

Good to know

Your login stays yours

The dashboard never asks for your Schoology password. It reads Schoology the way your browser already does once you're signed in.

Grades stay on your device

Nothing is sent to anyone. Clear data in settings removes everything it saved.

Questions or ideas?

Use Send feedback at the bottom of the dashboard. It opens your own email app.
